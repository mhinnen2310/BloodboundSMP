# BloodboundSMP P2/P3 Post-Launch Roadmap

These systems are intentionally scheduled after the 1.0 stability gate.

## P2 - 1.1+

- Blood Oaths, public marks, sold revenge contracts, Heat, Nemesis chains and Legacy collectibles.
- Player ticket workflow with staff comments and optional inventory snapshot references.
- Chronological staff-facing player timelines and inventory snapshot diff tools.
- Versioned data schemas, dry-run migrations, migration backups and rollback notes.
- Central validated settings registry and `/settings` management.
- Long-term hub/protection/Hall of Fame consolidation.
- Chat/command spam controls, join rate limiting and warnings-only bot detection.

## P3 - Later

- Rich EconomyWatch item metadata, supply/demand history and AH median pricing.
- Full anti-cheat review and punishment GUI.
- Expanded reports, tickets, player timeline, inventory diff and economy investigation GUIs.

The 1.0 release must remain focused on P0/P1 stability. These features require their own data-model, migration and multiplayer test passes.
